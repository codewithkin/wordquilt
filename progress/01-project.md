# What WordQuilt is

A daily themed word search where every hidden word belongs to one secret theme,
and **the letters you never use spell that theme out at the end.**

Drag a finger across adjacent letters to trace a word along a snaking path.
Words wind through the grid rather than sitting in straight lines. When the last
word is found, the remaining unused cells resolve in reading order and spell the
theme phrase.

**That reveal is the entire product. Everything else is scaffolding around it.**

---

## Who opens it

Someone who wants five quiet minutes with nothing asking anything of them.
Coffee, commute, bed. Skews mid-thirties and up, skews female, plays once a day,
and has likely uninstalled several competitors over ad frequency.

## The positioning

The word-puzzle category is enormous and almost entirely monetised through
aggressive advertising. A review analysis of 20 competitors found the
overwhelming majority of negative reviews were ad complaints — mid-game audio
ads, ads after every turn, forced level replay when players tried to skip an ad,
and one player who paid $5 for ad-free and still hit a currency wall.

WordQuilt is the inverse. The first line of the store listing is:

> **No ads. Not now, not later, not ever.**

With no marketing spend and no cross-promotion at launch, that promise is the
differentiator, the review-score engine, and the only real acquisition mechanism.

## The nine hard constraints

Each traces to a specific competitor failure. These are not preferences.

1. **No advertising anywhere, in any form.**
2. **No account, no login, no email capture.** Progress is on-device.
3. **Play is never gated by a resource** — no lives, energy, timers, currency.
   A puzzle must always be completable with zero hints.
4. **No punitive language ever** — no lost streaks, no broken chains, no
   "you missed a day", no red badges.
5. **Never repeat a puzzle.** A Daily can never reappear in a purchased pack,
   and vice versa.
6. **All prices in real currency.** No coins, no gems.
7. **Zero interruption budget on the Puzzle screen.** Nothing may appear over
   the grid during play — no modals, no toasts, no rating prompts, no purchase
   prompts.
8. **Three free hints daily**, refilling, counts labelled in plain words rather
   than unexplained icons.
9. **The contact route in Settings must actually work**, pre-filled with version
   and device info.

## Economy

Free to play, one-off purchases only. No subscription, no advertising, no
currency layer.

| Item | Contents | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theme Pack | 50 puzzles | $1.99 |
| Collection | 3 packs + 1 fabric set | $4.99 |
| Fabric set | Patterns, stitch colours, borders | $0.99–$2.99 |
| Hint bundle | 25 hints | $0.99 |
| Hint bundle | 100 hints | $2.99 |
| **The Whole Quilt** | All 5 launch packs + 3 fabric sets + 12 months of new packs | **$12.99** |

The economy deliberately shifts weight **off relief and onto stuff**. Selling
tools that make a game easier requires the game to be frustrating enough that
people want relief — which would mean manufacturing the exact anxiety the brand
exists to avoid. So cosmetics carry the repeat-purchase load, and hints are
generous enough that a normal player never needs to buy any.

## Content at launch

- **Free:** 6 themes × 20 puzzles = 120, plus one Daily Quilt, free forever,
  never gated, playable retroactively.
- **Paid:** 5 packs × 50 = 250.
- **Total:** 370 puzzles. One new 50-puzzle pack per month at $1.99; Whole Quilt
  owners get them free.

120 free was chosen deliberately: a binge player clears it in 2–4 days, a casual
daily player in about four weeks. Both hit the paywall while still enthusiastic,
which is the only moment that converts.

## The real launch risk: vocabulary

Not the packer, and not the design.

370 puzzles × ~8 words ≈ **3,000 word placements at launch.** No word repeats
inside a pack; no word appears in more than two packs. Each 50-puzzle theme
needs a curated pool of 200+ distinct, genuinely on-theme, common-usage words.
Some themes reach that comfortably ("Kitchen Things"). Some do not — "Rain"
became a 20-puzzle free theme for exactly this reason.

Every word must fall within roughly the **top 30,000 lemmas**. Difficulty comes
from grid size and path tangling, never from vocabulary obscurity. British and
American spellings are locked per locale rather than accepted inconsistently.

A player who reached level 2000 of a competitor still noticed the same handful
of words recurring. The most engaged players are the ones who catch it.

## Surfaces

**Onboarding — 9 screens.** Runs once, no skip control because nothing is
skippable. Reaches the aha moment in ~90 seconds, before anything is asked, and
ends with the player having already earned their first square. No sign-up
(there are no accounts) and no paywall (nothing is sold to someone who hasn't
played).

**Core — 10 surfaces.** Shelf (home), Puzzle, Reveal, Pack view, The Wall (the
sole conversion surface), Store, Daily calendar, Fabric, Hint overlay, Settings.
